Accessibility Statement

Last reviewed October 22, 2026

Our commitment

Everyone should be able to find our menu, plan a visit and book a table. We aim for this website to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA, the standard commonly used for the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA).

What we've done

  • A “Skip to main content” link and a logical heading structure for screen readers.
  • Text descriptions for every photo and video.
  • Every feature works with a keyboard alone, with a clearly visible focus outline.
  • Text and buttons meet AA color-contrast ratios.
  • Forms have labels, clear required fields and error messages that screen readers announce.
  • A “Pause motion” button stops moving and animated content. If you turn on “reduce motion” in your device settings, the site is calm by default.
  • The layout works on phones and when zoomed up to 400%.

Known limitations

The embedded Google Map is provided by a third party and may not be fully accessible. Our address and written directions are always available as text on the Visit section.

In the restaurant

[Confirm with the restaurant:] Our entrance, dining room and restrooms are wheelchair accessible. Service animals are welcome. Large-print menus are available on request.

Need help or found a problem?

If anything on this site is hard to use, please tell us. We'll help you book by phone right away and work to fix the problem.
