1. Who we are

Peruvian Soul (“we”, “us”) is a restaurant in downtown Frederick, Maryland. This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through this website and when you contact or book with us.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Reservation requests: name, email address, phone number (optional), date, time, party size, and any notes you choose to share, such as an occasion or food allergies.
  • Newsletter sign-up: your email address.
  • Messages: anything you send us by email or phone.

Allergy or dietary notes may be sensitive. Share only what our kitchen needs to serve you safely; we use it only for your visit.

Information collected automatically

  • Server logs: our web host records standard technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, to keep the site secure and running.
  • Browser storage: we store your cookie choice, and a short-lived counter used to block automated spam, in your browser's local storage.
  • Optional services: only if you allow them (see Cookies).

3. How we use it

  • To manage and confirm reservations and contact you about your booking.
  • To send our newsletter, if you signed up. Every email includes an unsubscribe link.
  • To answer your questions and improve our service.
  • To protect the site and our guests from fraud, spam and abuse.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

4. Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers who help us run the restaurant and this site, under contracts that limit how they can use it:

  • Our reservation system provider [name, e.g. OpenTable, Resy or Tock].
  • Our email newsletter provider [name].
  • Our website host [name].
  • Google, only if you choose to load the embedded map.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our guests, staff or business.

7. Your rights & choices

Depending on where you live, including under the Maryland Online Data Privacy Act, you may have the right to:

  • Find out what personal information we hold about you and get a copy.
  • Correct information that is wrong.
  • Ask us to delete your information.
  • Opt out of marketing emails at any time.
  • Appeal if we decline your request.

To make a request, contact us using the details below. We will respond within 45 days and will not treat you differently for using your rights.

8. Security

This site is served only over encrypted HTTPS connections. We limit access to personal information to staff who need it and use reputable providers. No system is perfectly secure, so please don't send payment card details by email.

9. Children

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information online.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the “Effective” date above and, for significant changes, post a notice on the site.
